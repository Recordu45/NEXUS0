Our Vision

NEXUS is being developed around a simple idea: instead of switching between many different apps for everyday tasks, users should have a single intelligent starting point.

The project is designed to grow gradually. Search, banking utilities, train information, emergency tools, nearby services, calculators, and other modules can be connected through one consistent NEXUS interface.
