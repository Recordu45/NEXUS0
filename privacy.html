Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NEXUS is intended to handle information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

NEXUS may process information that you voluntarily enter into the application, such as search queries, saved preferences, emergency contacts, or information required for a particular feature.

Some features may also use device permissions such as location, microphone, or telephone functions. These permissions are requested only when the relevant feature requires them.

2. Search Information

When you use the search feature, your search may be sent to an external search provider in order to return search results. NEXUS does not claim ownership of the external provider's services, content, or privacy practices.

3. Location Information

Nearby and emergency location features may request your device location. Location access is controlled by your device permissions. You can deny or revoke location access through your device settings.

4. Emergency Features

Emergency tools are provided as convenience features. NEXUS does not guarantee that an emergency call, message, location share, network connection, or third-party service will work at any particular time.

Always use official emergency services and follow local emergency procedures when immediate assistance is required.

5. Local Storage

Some NEXUS prototype features may store settings, recent activity, announcements, or preferences locally in your browser using local storage.

Clearing browser data may remove this locally stored information.

6. Third-Party Services

NEXUS may link to or use third-party services such as search engines, maps, railway information services, communication services, or other external platforms. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures should be used to protect information handled by NEXUS. However, no website, application, network, or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

NEXUS is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the service, appropriate action should be taken to remove it.

9. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as NEXUS develops. The latest version should always be treated as the applicable version published with the application.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, use the official contact method provided by the NEXUS owner or administrator.
